Privacy Policy

How Mini Girly Games handles your information.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stand Digital DOO (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects information in connection with the Mini Girly Games mobile application (the “App”). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

The App is owned and operated by Stand Digital DOO, Soloćuša bb, Srebrenica 75430, Republika Srpska, Bosnia and Herzegovina (VAT 405016490002). If you have any questions, you can reach us any time at contact@stand-digital.com.

2. Information we collect

We designed Mini Girly Games to be fun first and privacy-friendly. We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, address or phone number to play. Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be collected:

  • Gameplay & device data: basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, language, app version, crash reports and anonymous usage statistics that help us fix bugs and improve the games.
  • Advertising & analytics identifiers: if the App shows ads or uses analytics, our partners may process device identifiers to measure performance and, where permitted, show relevant ads.
  • Purchase information: if you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Apple or Google. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred but never your full payment card details.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply and provide support.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the App and its mini games.
  • To diagnose technical problems, fix crashes and keep the App secure.
  • To understand which games are popular so we can create more of what you love.
  • To show and measure advertising that keeps the App free to play.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.

4. Children’s privacy

Mini Girly Games is intended for a general audience and is meant to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that would require verifiable parental consent. Where the App is directed to children or used by children, we aim to limit data collection to what is necessary to run the App and, where ads are shown, to use only child-appropriate, non-personalized advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at contact@stand-digital.com and we will delete it.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us run the App, such as:

  • App stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) for downloads and purchases;
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers;
  • Advertising partners (where advertising is used);
  • Authorities, where required by law.

These providers may only use the information to perform services on our behalf.

6. Advertising choices

Where personalized ads are used, you can limit ad tracking through your device settings (for example, “Allow Apps to Request to Track” on iOS or “Ads” settings on Android).

7. Data retention & security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, and we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to safeguard your data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email us at contact@stand-digital.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App.
